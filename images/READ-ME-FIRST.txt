
PUT YOUR WEBSITE IMAGES IN THIS FOLDER
=======================================

FOLDER STRUCTURE
────────────────

images/
│
├── hero.JPG          ← The large opening background photo
│                       (full-screen image behind the title)
│
├── pool.JPG          ← Photo of your pool
│                       (shown in the Pool section)
│
├── about.JPG         ← Optional: a lifestyle photo for the About section
│
└── rooms/
    ├── room-01/      ← Photos for Room 1 (Pool View)
    │   ├── 1.JPG
    │   ├── 2.JPG
    │   └── 3.JPG     (add as many as you like)
    │
    ├── room-02/      ← Photos for Room 2 (Panorama Suite)
    │   ├── 1.JPG
    │   └── 2.JPG
    │
    ├── room-03/      ← Photos for Room 3 (Triple Panorama)
    │   └── 1.JPG
    │
    └── room-04/      ← Photos for Room 4 (Terrace Room)
        └── 1.JPG


RULES
─────
• Photo files MUST be named with numbers:  1.JPG  2.JPG  3.JPG  ...
• Supported formats: JPG  JPEG  PNG  WebP
• After adding photos, update the  image.count  number in site.config
  to match how many photos you uploaded.

EXAMPLE
───────
You upload 5 photos to  images/rooms/room-01/
You name them:  1.JPG   2.JPG   3.JPG   4.JPG   5.JPG

Then in  site.config  you set:
    room.1.image.count = 5

Refresh your browser — the photos appear automatically!
